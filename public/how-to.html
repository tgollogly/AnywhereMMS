📱 How to Use AnywhereMMS

No account. No app store. No tech skills needed.

All you need: your phone, mobile data or Wi‑Fi, and a chat app you already use (WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS, etc.).

Step 1 — Open the website

Ask whoever set this up for the link, or open the AnywhereMMS website in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).

💡 Tip: Bookmark the page or tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone so it works like an app.

Step 2 — Take or pick a photo

  1. Tap Take Photo and allow camera access, or
  2. Tap Choose from Gallery to pick an existing picture
  3. Tap Capture when you're happy with the shot

Step 3 — Get your link

  1. Make sure Get Link is selected (it's the easiest option)
  2. Tap the big Create Share Link button
  3. Wait a few seconds — your link appears

Step 4 — Send it to someone

Pick whichever is easiest for you:

📤

Share via Phone

Tap this and pick WhatsApp, Messages, Email, etc. from your phone's share menu.

📋

Copy

Tap Copy, open your chat app, paste the link, and send.

What the other person does

They just tap the link you sent. The photo opens in their browser — nothing to install, no login.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. AnywhereMMS is free to use. Normal mobile data or Wi‑Fi charges from your phone company may apply.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs in your phone's browser like a normal website.

How long does the link last?

Links expire after about 72 hours, then the photo is deleted automatically.

What about the "Auto-Send SMS" tab?

Ignore that unless someone technical set it up for you. Most people should use Get Link and paste it themselves.

Camera won't open?

Use Choose from Gallery instead, or check that your browser is allowed to use the camera in phone Settings.
