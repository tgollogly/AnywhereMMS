Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

AnywhereMMS ("we", "our", "the Service") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Photos you upload: Temporarily stored on our server to generate a view link for SMS delivery.
  • Phone numbers: Used solely to deliver the SMS containing the image link. We do not store phone numbers after sending.
  • Technical data: Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent) for security and abuse prevention.
  • Cookie preferences: Stored locally in your browser via localStorage.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Compress and temporarily host images for link-based sharing.
  • Send SMS messages through third-party SMS providers.
  • Prevent abuse via rate limiting.
  • Automatically delete expired images.

3. Data Retention

Images are automatically deleted after the configured retention period (default: 72 hours) or after reaching the maximum view count (default: 10 views). Server logs are retained for up to 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

SMS delivery may use third-party providers (e.g., TextBelt, Twilio). These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not share images with SMS providers — only the text message containing the link.

5. Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of an image before expiry by contacting the operator.
  • You should only send images to recipients who have consented to receive them.
  • You may decline non-essential cookies via our cookie banner.

6. Security

We use HTTPS, unique unguessable links, rate limiting, and automatic expiry. No system is 100% secure — use the Service at your own discretion for non-sensitive content.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
